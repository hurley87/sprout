# Sprout — Decisions and Open Questions

Product direction recorded on 2026-10-30, with a follow-up on 2026-11-03.

## November 3 direction and follow-up

- **User direction:** create fun learning experiences beyond mathematics; characters are optional; consider girls and boys and investigate the appropriate age range.
- **Requested work:** research Markdown files followed by a concrete first curriculum/catalog deliverable.
- **Delivered proposal:** [research](research/01-head-start-framework.md), [ten-entry catalog](10-experience-catalog.md), [skill map](11-skill-map-and-progression.md), three worked experiences, and a [pilot plan](12-pilot-and-validation.md).
- **Still hypotheses:** ages 3–6, the initial 3–5 minute pilot target, recommendation rules, activity ordering, and experience-led navigation. Ages 3–11 are not a validated audience claim.
- **Still to validate:** independent child use, the teaching designs, voice reliability across children, transfer/retention, and willingness to return or pay.

The November 3 user direction supersedes the math-only and mandatory-character framing below. Other November 3 design proposals remain reviewable hypotheses. The dated earlier record is retained for provenance.

## Remaining product questions

### Characters and visual execution

- How can characters become familiar without encouraging unhealthy emotional dependence?
- How should the hand-drawn reference translate into Pip, backgrounds, and animated reactions?
- Which animation method preserves the illustrated look while synchronizing with speech?

### Lessons and progression

- What is the first authored sequence of math levels and its learning objectives?
- Does each level represent a whole lesson, or do multiple short levels fit within one 5–10 minute lesson?
- What evidence advances a level, and how do assisted and independent responses affect later practice?
- How much conversational freedom should fit within each structured lesson?
- Where within the 5–10 minute range do observed engagement and useful practice balance best?
- How should replay, continuing to another lesson, and natural stopping points work?

### Voice-only play and adult handoff

- What spoken choices and requests should the child be able to use naturally?
- How should Sprout handle hesitation, silence, unclear speech, and interruptions?
- How should the session recover after connection loss or request adult help when recovery fails?
- What setup and end-of-session handoff make it practical for the adult to step away?

### Technology and evidence

- What timing signals should drive speech-synchronized animation?
- What learning evidence is reliable enough to show parents?
- What is the cost per useful learning session?

### Business

- What benefit makes parents subscribe?
- Is the character experience a meaningful differentiator?
- What causes families to return after the novelty fades?
- What acquisition channels can work profitably?
- What evidence would justify expanding beyond the initial math focus?
