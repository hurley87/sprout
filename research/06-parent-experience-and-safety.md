# Sprout — Parent Experience and Safety

Parent controls, summaries, future features, and proposed safety and privacy principles.

**Status:** Early product exploration and prototyping.

Based on sections 10 of the [original product exploration](source/product-exploration.md), updated with the user’s 2026-10-30 product direction.

## Parent Experience

The parent experience is a separate part of the product.

### Adult setup, independent child play

An adult is present to set up and start the lesson. Once the lesson begins, the adult can step away while the child learns. Continuous adult participation is not the intended product requirement.

The child must be able to follow instructions, respond, and move between levels using voice alone. The lesson should not depend on an adult reading labels or operating controls during routine play.

The working duration target is 5–10 minutes per lesson. When the lesson finishes, it should provide a clear ending and a parent-readable recap. The exact behavior for unrecoverable errors or a child asking for an adult remains to be designed.

### Initial features

- Parent-controlled child profile.
- Subject-first selection, starting with Math, and age-appropriate lesson setup.
- Session duration controls.
- Recent activities.
- Simple learning summaries.
- Suggested offline practice.
- Privacy and data controls.

### Future possibilities

- Learning goals and progress over time.
- Multiple children under one subscription.
- Recommendations based on demonstrated skills.
- Optional progress notifications.

### Safety and privacy

- Transparent that instructors are AI characters.
- Parent-controlled setup and access, followed by independent voice-only play within the structured lesson.
- No unrestricted conversations or requests for personal information.
- Data minimization and clearly defined retention.
- Appropriate parental consent.
- No third-party advertising inside children's learning sessions.
- Do not depend on selling children's personal learning data.
- Review applicable children's privacy and platform requirements before deployment.
